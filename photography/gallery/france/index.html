France

France is my home country and the place I have photographed the most. Most of my pictures were taken here, switching from cityscapes, as in Bordeaux or Saint-Émilion, to landscapes. I tried to immortalize well-known places, such as Mont Saint-Michel, the cliffs of Étretat in Normandy and the coast of Brittany, from Finistère to Morbihan, where I keep going back for its beauty.

Exploring the less popular corners of the Hexagon is another pleasure: quiet places that deserve to be known, like the infamous lake of Guizengeard or the homey fishing port of Saint-Goustan. Seascapes became a genre of their own for me and took me on several trips along the West and South Coasts.

The whims of the weather, from the unbearable heat in the lavender fields of Valensole to the freezing cold from the sea at the top of the cliffs, make, without a doubt, for great stories, and for the beauty of this art.
