About this Gallery

Most of my pictures were taken here, switching from cityscapes, as in Bordeaux, Toulouse, or Saint-Émilion, to landscapes. I tried to immortalize well-known places, such as Mont Saint-Michel, the cliffs of Étretat and the countryside of Fond-Pitron in Normandy, and the coast of Brittany, from Finistère to Morbihan, where I keep going back for its beauty.

Exploring the less popular corners of the Hexagon is another pleasure: quiet places that deserve to be known, like the infamous lake of Guizengeard, the plateau of Aubrac, or the homey fishing port of Saint-Goustan. Seascapes became a genre of their own for me and took me on several trips along the West Coast and to Charente-Maritime.

The whims of the weather, from the unbearable heat in the lavender fields of Valensole to the freezing cold from the sea at the top of the cliffs, make, without a doubt, for great stories, and for the beauty of this art.
