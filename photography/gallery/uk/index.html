UK

The United Kingdom has a special place in my life, and in my photography. I lived in this wonderful country for six years, mostly in London, a fantastic playground within my reach where I shot many cityscapes: the monuments along the River Thames, St Paul's Cathedral, and icons known worldwide like Big Ben and the London Eye. Now and then I escaped the birthplace of the Rolling Stones for Hyde Park, where nature took over from architecture in my pictures.

I still go back to the country regularly, mostly to the north, for the unique countryside of the Lake District and for the gorgeous landscapes of Scotland, around Glencoe and Glenfinnan.
