Finland

An invitation took me to Finland during a summer. From its capital, Helsinki, to the famous Archipelago, I was lucky to see the summer nights, when the sky is never fully dark and the blue hour stretches on for a while.

The light is like nowhere else, and the city's buildings, rich in history, sit in a mood somewhere between sunset and blue hour.
