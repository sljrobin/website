Spain

Spain is where my photography, mostly cityscapes until then, turned to landscapes. Having decided it was time to dive into the genre, I dedicated a whole road trip to it and drove along the Northern Coast, from the flysch of Zumaia to the beaches of Cantabria, with a stop at the iconic San Juan de Gaztelugatxe.

New challenges came with this adventure: planning the shots, reading the weather, getting up while the sun is still asleep, or standing in the water for hours to capture a sunset.

Those are the moments when you realize how hard taking pictures can be, and how much patience gives back.
