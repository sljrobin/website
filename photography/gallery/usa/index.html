USA

My journey in photography starts here, in Washington state. A few days before leaving for Seattle, I decided to take photography seriously and bought a mirrorless camera: I knew I would not fly to this country again for a while, so the occasion was perfect. This trip taught me the basics of cityscapes, chasing the good light and trying different exposures.

Besides Pike Place Market, the Aquarium and the ports with their beautiful sailing boats, I tried to capture the magnificence of the iconic Space Needle, which I was lucky enough to admire from my workplace, and from Kerry Park at sunset and blue hour.
