About this Gallery

I lived in this wonderful country for six years, mostly in London, a fantastic playground within my reach where I shot many cityscapes: the monuments along the River Thames, St Paul’s Cathedral, and icons known worldwide like the London Eye and Tower Bridge. Now and then I escaped the birthplace of the Rolling Stones for Hyde Park, where nature took over from architecture in my pictures, or for the seaside at Brighton.

I still go back to the country regularly, mostly to the north, for the unique countryside of the Lake District, from Derwentwater and Langdale to Grasmere and the fields of Culgaith, and for the gorgeous landscapes of Scotland, around Glencoe, Cuil Bay, and Glenfinnan.
