Switzerland

Switzerland is a country I had the chance to visit a couple of times.

Zürich

Geneva

About this Gallery

From Zürich and its churches to Geneva, with the Jet d’Eau on its lake, this country clearly has an architecture of its own, not seen in the rest of Europe, with buildings in the Gothic and Renaissance styles.

Surrounded by lakes and mountains, it has always struck me as a place where natural elements find their way into a cityscape.
