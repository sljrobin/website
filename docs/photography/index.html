The Photographer

Who takes these pictures, and where to see more.

Besides my work, I enjoy taking pictures. I’m drawn to cityscapes and landscapes, with a soft spot for Normandy and Brittany in France. I took up photography seriously in 2017, on my way to Seattle in the USA, and I’ve been chasing light ever since.

If you’re curious, I’ve written about how my journey started, the photographers who inspire me, and the equipment I carry on my trips in Behind the Camera.

You can also find me on Instagram, where I post now and then: sljrobin
