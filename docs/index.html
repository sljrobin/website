Hi, I’m Simon

Over 10 years of offensive security. I test cloud environments and Kubernetes clusters from the attacker’s side.

I’m Simon Robin, a Senior Security Consultant at IOActive. I find out how cloud-native infrastructure fails under real attack, and how to design and operate it so that it doesn’t. I also test web and mobile applications and networks, review code, and build threat models.

This site is where I keep track of what I write, the talks I give, and what I do off the clock.

I’m always glad to talk about security. You’ll find me on several networks, but LinkedIn is where I usually answer.
